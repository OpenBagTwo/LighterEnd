**Aurant Polypores** are a type of [shelf fungus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polypore)
and a light-emitting decorative block.

## Obtaining

Aurant Polypore blocks do not naturally generate.

They can be broken without tools.

See: [item entry](../Items/aurant_polypore#obtaining).

## Usage

Aurant Polypores can be placed on the sides of full blocks as decoration. They also provide a
light level of 13.

See also: [item entry](../Items/aurant_polypore#usage).
