**Foggy Agave Fur** is a fluffy plant fiber that grows on the sides of
[Foggy Agave Bulbs](Blocks/blue_vine_lantern).

## Obtaining

Foggy Agave Fur naturally generate as part of
[Foggy Agave plants](Items/blue_vine_seed)
in [Foggy Mushroomland](biomes#foggy-mushroomland) biomes, attached to
[Foggy Agave Bulbs](Blocks/blue_vine_lantern).

## Usage

See: [item entry](Items/blue_vine_fur)

Foggy Agave Fur can be placed on any side of a solid block as decoration.
