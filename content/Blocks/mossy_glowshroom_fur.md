**Glowshroom Fur** is a fluffy plant fiber that grows on the underside of
[Mossy Glowshrooms](Items/mossy_glowshroom_sapling).

## Obtaining

Glowshroom Fur naturally generates on the undersides of
[Mossy Glowshrooms](Items/mossy_glowshroom_sapling)
in [Foggy Mushroomland](biomes#foggy-mushroomland) biomes, attached to
the sides of [Glowshroom Hymenophores](blocks/mossy_glowshroom_hymenophore).

## Usage

See: [item entry](Items/mossy_glowshroom_fur)

Glowshroom Fur can be placed on any side of a solid block as decoration.
