**Silk Moth Nests** are naturally generating and craftable blocks that house [Silk Moths](Mobs/silk_moth).

## Obtaining

Silk Moth Nests naturally generate in [Blossom Forest](biomes#blossom-forest) biomes under [Tenanea Leaves](Blocks/tenanea_leaves).

When broken by hand or with a tool not enchanted with [Silk Touch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silk_Touch), they drop nothing
and release any moth inside.

When broken using a Silk Touch tool, they will drop while preserving the state of their occupant along with their fullness level.

They can also be crafted. See: [item entry](Items/silk_moth_nest).

## Usage

[Silk Moths](Mobs/silk_moth) will try to find unoccupied nests to enter. After spending 2400 game ticks (two minutes) inside,
the moths will exit and increase the nest's fullness level by one.

Once the Nest reaches its maximum fullness level of 3, it can be sheared, resetting its fullness level and dropping three
pieces of [Silk Fiber](Items/silk_fiber).
