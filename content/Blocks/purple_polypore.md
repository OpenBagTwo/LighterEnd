**Purple Polypores** are a type of [shelf fungus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polypore)
and a light-emitting decorative block.

## Obtaining

Purple Polypores naturally generate on the bark of [Dragon Trees](dragon_tree_log) in the
[Shadow Forest](../biomes#shadow-forest). They are non-renewable, generating only during worldgen.

They can be broken without tools.

## Usage

Purple Polypores can be placed on the sides of full blocks as decoration.

See also: [item entry](../Items/purple_polypore#usage).
