**Purple Charnia** is a variety of underwater [frond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charnia)
found in End Lakes and Geysers.

## Obtaining

All varieties of [Charnia](tags/charnia) naturally generate at the bottoms of End Lakes
(including [Megalakes](biomes#megalake)),
Sulfur Lakes and Geysers.

They can also be grown by using [Bone Meal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_Meal) underwater in The End,
though that can be disabled in the Lighter End config, accessed either through
[ModMenu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) or by editing `config/lighterend.yaml`
in your Minecraft folder and setting the line:

```yaml
bonemealing_underwater_in_the_end_produces_end_vegetation: false
```
