**Silk Moth Nests** are naturally generating and craftable blocks that house [Silk Moths](Mobs/silk_moth).

## Obtaining

Silk Moth Nests naturally generate in [Blossom Forest](biomes#blossom-forest) biomes. See [block entry](Blocks/silk_moth_nest).

They can also be crafted by combining six [Tenanea Planks](Items/tenanea_planks) with one [Silk Matrix](Items/silk_matrix)

![Crafting a Silk Matrix into a Moth Nest](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_moth_nest.png)
