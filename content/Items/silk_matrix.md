**Silk Matrices** are compacted versions of [Silk Fiber](Items/silk_fiber) used to craft [Silk Elytra](Items/silk_elytra)
and [Silk Moth Nests](Blocks/silk_moth_nest).

## Obtaining

Nine pieces of [Silk Fiber](Items/silk_fiber) can be crafted in a grid to produce one Silk Matrix

![Crafting a Silk Matrix from Silk Fiber](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_matrix.png)

## Usage

Silk Matrices can be broken back into [Silk Fiber](silk_fiber), providing nine Fibers for every Matrix.

![Crafting a Silk Matrix into a Moth Nest](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_moth_nest.png)

Combining one Silk Matrix and six [Tenanea Planks](Items/tenanea_planks) produces one [Silk Moth Nest](Items/silk_moth_nest).

![Crafting a Silk Elytra](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_elytra.png)

Combining six Silk Matrices and two [Phantom Membranes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom_Membrane) produces
a [Silk Elytra](silk_elytra)
