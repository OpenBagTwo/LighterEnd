**Silk Fiber** is a material produced by [Silk Moths](Mobs/silk_moth).

## Obtaining

Silk Fiber can be obtained by shearing full [Silk Moth Nests](Blocks/silk_moth_nest) or by
killing [Silk Moths](Mobs/silk_moth).

## Usage

Silk Fiber can be broken down to provide two pieces of [String](https://minecraft.wiki/w/String)
per piece of Silk Fiber.

Alternatively, nine Silk Fibers can be crafted in a grid to produce one [Silk Matrix](Items/silk_matrix).

![Crafting a Silk Matrix from Silk Fiber](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_matrix.png)

Silk Fiber can also be used to repair damaged [Silk Elytra](Items/silk_elytra).
