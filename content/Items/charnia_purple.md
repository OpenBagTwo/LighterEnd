**Purple Charnia** is a variety of underwater [frond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charnia)
found in End Lakes and Geysers.

## Obtaining

See: [block entry](Blocks/charnia_purple)

All varieties of [Charnia](tags/charnia) must be broken with [Shears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shears)
or a tool with [Silk Touch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silk_Touch). Otherwise, Charnia blocks drop nothing.

## Usage

Cooking Purple Charnia in a [Furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace),
[End Stone Furnace](Blocks/end_stone_furnace),
[Smoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smoker) or
[End Stone Smoker](Blocks/end_stone_smoker) yields one [Purple Dye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Purple_Dye)
per Charnia.

Purple Charnia can be used as [Fletching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fletching) in the crafting of
[Spectral Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectral_Arrow).
