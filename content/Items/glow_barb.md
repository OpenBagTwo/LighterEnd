**Glob Barbs** are [nematocysts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cnidocyte)
that give [Cubozoas](Mobs/cubozoa) their painful sting and can
be crafted into [Spectral Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectral_Arrow).

## Obtaining

Glow Barbs can be obtained by killing [Cubozoas](Mobs/cubozoa)

## Usage

![Crafting recipe for Spectral Arrows](assets/items/extra/crafting_spectral_arrows.png)

Combining one Glow Barb, one [Stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stick) and one [Fletching](tags/fletching) item
in a crafting table produces four [Spectral Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectral_Arrow).
