**Silk Elytra** is a chest-equippable [glide item](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elytra) that also provides
armor protection.

## Obtaining

![Crafting a Silk Elytra](assets/items/extra/crafting_silk_elytra.png)

To craft a Silk Elytra, combine six [Silk Matrices](Items/silk_matrix) with two
[Phantom Membranes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom_Membrane).

Heavily damaged Silk Elytra can also be found extremely rarely through [fishing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fishing)
in The End.

## Usage

Silk Elytra can be dyed any color using any combination of [Dyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dye).
Using a dyed Silk Elytra with a full
[Cauldron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cauldron) will return it to its original color.
Silk Elytras can also be [trimmed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smithing#Trimming).

> [!info] Note
> By default, trimmed silk elytras will show the trims
> on both the chest and the wing layer. However, the wing
> layer trims can be disabled by going into your **Resource Pack**
> settings and removing the **"Wing Trims"** pack.
>
> ![Demonstration of where to click to disable wing trimming](assets/config/disabling_wing_trims.png)

Silk Elytra can be enchanted, either in an [Enchantment Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanting_Table)
or using an [Anvil](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Anvil#Enchanted_books), and are compatible with all body
enchantments.

Un-enchanted, Silk Elytra provide 8 points of armor (the same as Diamond and Netherite).

Silk Elytra have 100 points of durability, which can consumed either through taking damage or by flying.

Silk Elytra can be repaired in an anvil using [Silk Fiber](Items/silk_fiber) or by combining with another
damaged Silk Elytra.
