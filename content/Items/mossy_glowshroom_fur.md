**Glowshroom Fur** is a fluffy plant fiber that grows on the underside of
[Mossy Glowshrooms](Items/mossy_glowshroom_sapling).

## Obtaining

See: [block entry](Blocks/mossy_glowshroom_fur)

Glowshroom Fur must be broken with [Shears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shears)
or a tool with [Silk Touch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silk_Touch). Otherwise, the fur block drops nothing.

## Usage

Glowshroom Fur can be equipped in a player's head slot as a silly-looking
ornamentation. A player wearing Glowshroom Fur can look directly at an
[Enderman](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman) without angering them.

![Player wearing Glowshroom Fur](assets/items/extra/wearing_glowshroom_fur.png)

Glowshroom Fur can also be used as a [Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion) ingredient:
infusing one into an [Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion) yields
a Potion of [End Veil](misc#end_veil).

Glowshroom Fur can be used as [Fletching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fletching) in the crafting of
[Spectral Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectral_Arrow).

![Crafting recipe for Spectral Arrows](assets/items/extra/crafting_spectral_arrows.png)
