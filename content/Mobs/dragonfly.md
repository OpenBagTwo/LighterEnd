**Dragonflies** are passive mobs found in [Glowing Grasslands](biomes#glowing-grassland), [Umbrella Jungles](biomes#umbrella-jungle)
and [Foggy Mushroomlands](biomes#foggy-mushroomland).

They drop nothing when killed.
