**End Slimes** are hostile mobs found in [Umbrella Jungles](../biomes#umbrella-jungle) and [Foggy Mushroomlands](../biomes#foggy-mushroomland).

As with their [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime) and [Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_Cube) counterparts,
when a large End Slime is killed, it splits into smaller End Slimes.

Small End Slimes drop [End Cream](../items/end_cream) upon death.

> [!note] Changed from Better End
> This is different from Better End, where End Slimes dropped Slime Balls.
