**Chorus Crabs** are a neutral mob found primarily in [Blossoming Forests](../biomes#blossoming-forest), but which can
also be found along the shores of [Megalakes](../biomes#megalake).

They show no interest in players by default but will defend themselves if attacked, using their massive claws to
deal massive knockback (though not much actual damage).

Chorus Crabs can be bred using [Chorus Flowers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chorus_Flower), and baby Chorus Crabs
will ride on their parent's back until reaching maturity.

Chorus Crabs will also pick up—and wield—swords they find on the ground. They also very rarely spawn being ridden by
[Enderman](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman) Jockeys.

Upon death, Chorus Crabs drop [Raw Crab Meat](../items/crab_meat) and [Crab Claws](../items/crab_claw).
